App concept · Internal draft
One app for TriDot and RunDot athletes

The science you have.
The experience it deserves.

This concept starts from what TriDot and RunDot already do, based on their current App Store screens, descriptions and athlete reviews. It reorganizes those features around one question every athlete opens the app with: what should I do today, and why?

All names, workouts, scores and times on these screens are illustrative sample data. Each screen is tagged to show what exists in the current apps today and what is a new concept that would need product and engineering review.

01One plan, every disciplineRunning and triathlon live in one home. More endurance sports can slot in later without a new app.
02Today comes firstOpen the app and see one clear session, one primary action, then everything else.
03Explain the whyFitLogic already knows why a session is on the plan. Say it in one plain sentence.
04Fewer numbers, better onesKeep Dot Score, TrainX and stress, but give each one a meaning and a single home.
05Built for real lifeMoving a session, a hot day or a missed week should feel calm, never like failure.

05 · Reality check

What it would take

Most of this concept is a redesign of features that already exist. These are the items that need product and engineering input before anyone commits.

AreaStatusOpen question
One app for both sportsNewMerge two codebases, or extend one? Plans, history and login carry over. Subscriptions are unchanged for now.
"Why today" lineNewCan FitLogic expose the reason behind each session? Would it be written by coaches, generated, or both?
Plan-ready recapNewUses onboarding inputs we already collect. Mostly design and front-end work.
Move-a-session previewNewMoving sessions exists. Showing the knock-on effect before confirming needs a "what if" from the plan engine.
RaceX for runnersVerifyFull RaceX is a TriDot feature. RunDot has Add a Race and predicted finish.
Assistant actionsNewThe Assistant answers questions today. Letting it change the plan needs guardrails and confirmation steps.
Beginner safetyGapA 1-star RunDot review says the first sessions ramped too fast for a true beginner. Should onboarding offer a walk-run start? Coaching team to review.
ReliabilityGapA recent review reports workouts that won't start or that stop mid-session. Polish only lands if the basics are rock solid.
Strength and mobilityVerifyRunna leans on strength, pilates and mobility. Confirm what we offer today before showing it.
