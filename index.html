The scienceyou already trust.

The tools RunDot and TriDot athletes know by name, explained in plain language and now in one endurance platform.

  • AssessDot Score™

    A benchmark of your true performance ability, normalized for age, gender, and conditions.

  • PrescribeNormalized Training Stress®

    Quantifies the physiological stress of every session, so training load is measured, not guessed.

  • PrescribeEnviroNorm®

    Sets intensities for temperature, humidity, and elevation, so progress shows up in real conditions, not just good-weather days.

  • EvaluateTrainX®

    Scores how well you executed what was prescribed and tracks your consistency over time.

  • PredictRaceX®

    Forecasts race-day paces and splits using course terrain, temperature, humidity, and elevation.

  • ConnectYour devices

    Works with the watches you already train with, including Garmin, COROS, and Apple Watch.

Feature names carried over from RunDot and TriDot. Availability may vary by training path and plan.
