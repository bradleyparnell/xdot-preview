01

Brand idea

Who we are

Endurance is something
you build.

XDOT is personalized endurance training for every endurance athlete. It brings RunDot and TriDot together in one app, built from each athlete's goals and data.

We start with running and triathlon, with more endurance sports on the way. The science behind the plans carries forward from more than 20 years of training data. Plans and training history move with the athlete.

Personal

Every plan is built from the athlete's own goals, schedule and data. Never a template.

Proven

Two decades of training science and 50,000+ athletes behind it. We show the proof and skip the hype.

Forward

Nothing left behind. Everything moving forward. One home that grows with the athlete and the sport.

Clear space and size

Keep space around the stacked logo equal to the height of the X in the wordmark. Nothing else enters that space.

Smallest sizes: the stacked logo at 72 px wide on screen (20 mm in print), the wordmark at 64 px wide, and the emblem at 16 px for favicons.

Gold is earned

One gold moment per view: a button, a highlighted word or the dot. Gold headline words appear only in the hero and the closing call to action.

Keep it flat

05

Photography

Golden hour, real athletes

Real people training for real goals, graded warm and calm. The feeling is early morning or late evening, focused and earned.

Do

  • Use real athletes from our own shoots first.
  • Apply the golden hour grade: warm highlights, about 45% less saturation, deep shadows.
  • Keep heads and faces fully in frame.
  • Show effort, focus and recovery, not just podium moments.
  • Add a light film grain on large photos.

Avoid

  • Prominent third-party logos and club kits.
  • Mirrored photos that flip logos or text.
  • Bright, saturated stock imagery or studio lighting.
  • Generic AI images that show recognizable people, signs or logos. AI scenes are for backgrounds only and should be labeled internally.
  • Any athlete photo without a release that covers XDOT.

We sound like a great coach: clear about the next step, honest about the work, never loud about ourselves.

Words we avoid

AdaptsHybrid athleteRevolutionaryGuaranteedCrushInjury-freeHacks

Short sentences

One idea per sentence. No long dashes. Headlines in two short lines.

Proof over promises

Real numbers, real athletes, real quotes. No invented stats, testimonials, guarantees or injury claims.

Reward the plan

Celebrate consistency and recovery, never extra volume. Streaks pause for illness and travel.
